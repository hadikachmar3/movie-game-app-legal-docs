# CineShelf privacy policy

**Effective date:** 25 July 2026

**Contact:** [coding.with.hadi@gmail.com](mailto:coding.with.hadi@gmail.com)

CineShelf ("the app") is developed and operated by Hadi Kachmar.

## 1. Overview

CineShelf helps you discover, organise, rate, review, and share movies,
television series, and video games. This policy explains what information the
app processes, why, and the choices you have.

## 2. Information we process

- **Account information** — your email address, Firebase user ID, display
  name, unique username, sign-in provider, and optional profile photo.
- **Library information** — your favourites, watch later and wishlist entries,
  watched and played entries, ownership state, personal ratings, dates,
  recommendations, and optional private comments.
- **Custom movies you create** — title, artwork URL, year, runtime, genres,
  rating, overview, and private notes.
- **Social information** — friend requests, friendships, blocked users,
  notifications, your privacy settings, and the library sections you choose to
  share.
- **Technical information** — the request data required to authenticate you and
  operate the service securely.

CineShelf does not upload poster images to our servers. When you save artwork,
the image is written to your own device.

## 3. How we use it

- To authenticate you and protect your account.
- To store and synchronise your library and preferences across your devices.
- To provide profiles, friend relationships, and the sharing you opt into.
- To retrieve movie, television, and game information.
- To prevent unauthorised access and diagnose failures.

**We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for
third-party advertising.**

## 4. Visibility and sharing

You control whether each profile section is private, visible to friends, or
public. Only minimal copies of the entries in a section are published, and only
for sections that are not private.

**Your private comments and private notes are never shared.** They remain in
your own private storage and are not copied into any social or public view.

Your display name, username, avatar, and anything else you place on a
discoverable profile may be visible to other signed-in users, subject to your
privacy settings and to blocking.

## 5. Service providers

CineShelf relies on:

- **Google Firebase Authentication** and **Cloud Firestore** — accounts and data
  storage.
- **Google Sign-In** — optional sign-in method.
- **Cloudflare Workers** — authenticated server-side API proxy.
- **TMDB** — movie and television information.
- **Twitch / IGDB** — video-game information.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.
Catalogue searches are sent through our Cloudflare proxy. We do not send your
private library comments to TMDB or IGDB.

## 6. Retention

Your account and library data is kept while your account is active. When you
delete your account, the data listed in our
[account deletion page](account-deletion.md) is removed immediately and cannot
be recovered.

Deleted data may persist briefly in encrypted infrastructure backups before
being overwritten in the normal backup rotation. It is not accessible in the
app or to other users during that time.

## 7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data at any time from **Profile → Settings
→ Delete account** in the app, or by emailing us.

Full instructions: [Delete your CineShelf account](account-deletion.md)

## 8. Security

We use authenticated Firebase access, server-side security rules, HTTPS, and an
authenticated proxy. No system can guarantee absolute security. Please protect
your credentials and report suspected account abuse to
[coding.with.hadi@gmail.com](mailto:coding.with.hadi@gmail.com).

## 9. Age requirement

CineShelf is not directed to children and is rated 16+. It is intended for
users aged 16 and over.

The app catalogues movies, television series, and video games supplied by
third-party providers. That catalogue can include artwork and descriptions
referencing violence, sexual content, strong language, and drug use. CineShelf
does not stream or play any of that content; it displays cover artwork and
written summaries only. Pornographic titles are excluded.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you
believe someone under 16 has created an account, contact us and we will delete
it.

## 10. International use

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own,
including outside your region of residence. By using CineShelf you understand
that your information may be transferred to and processed in those countries.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or
delete your personal information. You can exercise deletion directly in the app,
and you can contact us for any other request.

## 11. Changes

If we make material changes we will update the effective date above and, where
required, notify you in the app.
